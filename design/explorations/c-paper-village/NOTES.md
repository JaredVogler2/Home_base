# C — Paper Village

**Concept.** A pop-up-book street in layered cut paper; each building is one module wearing a literal sign. Child modules on the street (lower 60%), family ones behind, grown-ups on the hilltop.

**Palette.** Sky `#CBE5E3`, cream `#FFF6E2`, terracotta `#E8704F`, mustard `#F3B62F`, leaf `#7FBA6F`, teal `#40A0A0`, pink `#F299AC`, kraft `#CC9D63`, ink `#3A2B45`; shadows `#4A2D50`.

**Guide.** Bramble, a hedgehog postie cut with pinking shears, pointing with a flag.

**Status without text.** Board: mission cards, gold tick when done. Pig: stars in fives, hollow dots to the next. Clock: hand on sun or moon; window shows the next step. School: door ajar, flag up. Calendar: star today, heart ahead. Gallery: sparkle on the newest. Stall: basket shows what is wanted. Players: active badge larger, ringed, starred.

**Strengths.** Literal, warm, big targets low down; shadows read as pressable.

**Weaknesses.** Pastel page: weaker in glare, too bright at bedtime (needs night art). Similar façades; meaning rides on signs. Street is full: little room to grow. Village is a step removed from home.

**Performance.** One 135 KB file; one static shadow filter (11 uses); 22 transform-only loops, none filtered, off under reduced motion. SVG repaints on the main thread: layer movers in production.
