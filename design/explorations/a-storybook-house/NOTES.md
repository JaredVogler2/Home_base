# Direction A — Storybook House

**Concept.** HOMEBASE is literally a home: a cutaway storybook house under a living sky. Each room is one module and
the whole room is the button. Child-led rooms are on the ground floor (mission board, the day clock, the piggy bank,
the pantry basket); family-led rooms are upstairs (photo wall, calendar); the grown-ups' door is the round attic
window, out of a small child's reach. The boat on the pond sails to the Academy's lighthouse, seen far off across
the water. The sky, the windows and the lamps follow the household's real time of day (`?night`).

**Palette.** ink `#3a2e4d` · cream `#fff6e6` · honey `#ffc94d` (stars, "this way") · terracotta `#f08a62` ·
sage `#8fd08a` · pond teal `#62bfc4` · bluebird `#3f8fd1` · rose `#f594b4` · lavender `#b3a2f2`.

**Guide.** "Tock", the bluebird who lives behind the little door of the day clock (a cuckoo-clock bird): he comes
out to say what is next and flies to whatever he is pointing at. Peach breast, cocked tail, crest.

**Status without text.** Mission cards pinned on the board with a star socket for each (filled = done); the clock's
day wheel and the picture of what comes next; star tokens in a frame of ten beside the piggy; the things in the
basket; today's dot and pictures on the calendar; the photos on the wall.

**Strengths.** Rooms map to real places in a child's home, so nothing has to be learned; six large targets on a
plain grid, child modules lowest; warm at night without going dark; one arrangement re-flows to a tall phone.
**Weaknesses.** More detail per tile than the other directions, so each room must stay "few and large"; the house
is less exotic than a space station. **Cost.** Static vector, about a dozen small transform/opacity animations,
no filters.
