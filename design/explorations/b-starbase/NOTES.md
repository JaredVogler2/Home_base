# Direction B — Starbase Home

**Concept.** Home as a snug station on a tiny garden planet: warm, lit pods against a dusk sky. Each pod is a bright stage for one picture-object. The sky tells the time (`index.html#morning` shows dawn).

**Palette.** Night `#161d4a`→`#5a4c9c` · cream hull `#fffaf0`/`#e6d3b3` · glow `#fff3b8`/`#f4ad3c` · coral `#e8644c` · mint `#3aa584` · aqua `#3f93d6` · lilac `#7f63da` · rose `#ea6f9d` · ink `#1b2150`.

**Guide.** Nim, a star-lamb in a bubble helmet with a star aerial: hovers, points a hoof, blinks.

**Status without text.** Missions: picture cards; tick = done, empty ring = to do. Bank: star tokens in rows of five, empty places dim. Next: moon dial, play-badged toothbrush card, five step beads. Basket: three visible things. Calendar: today coral, one starred day. Shuttle: lit porthole. Grown-ups: keyhole hatch at the mast top.

**Strengths.** Strongest object contrast; calm and dim at bedtime; original.

**Weaknesses.** Dark sky mirrors a bright room (dawn palette helps); space is less literal than a house for shoes and toothbrushes; pods share one shell, told apart only by contents.

**Performance.** One 46 KB file, no script, no filters; 621 nodes, 22 transform/opacity loops; steady 16.7 ms frames at 3840×2160 in a software renderer (not the device).
