# 09 — MEMORIES: 4K family photo frame

Prefer Apolosign native Google Photos screensaver. Current support documents native screensaver including Google Photos in Android Mode: https://support.apolosign.com/hc/en-us/articles/56804488060825-How-to-Set-Up-Your-Screen-Saver. Configure parent-selected safe shared album, orientation, photo fill/fit, idle timeout, sleep schedule and brightness. Confirm whether screensaver starts over browser/kiosk mode on the purchased firmware, wakes back to correct HOMEBASE screen, and whether Android/kiosk screen lock, power saving or settings conflict. Test actual 4K output, image quality, cropping, album updates, sleep/wake and return. Do not assume videos from Google Photos are supported. No undocumented native photo API.

Create HOMEBASE Memories tile and fallback optional in-app idle slideshow from parent-selected media with 4K-capable responsive srcsets, modest prefetch, correct aspect ratio and orientation, face-safe crop only if user chooses, gentle crossfade, clock overlay, tap to return, quiet hours/night dimming, cache budget and no external autoplay. For custom Google Photos integration, use current official Picker API and policy review: https://developers.google.com/photos/picker/guides/get-started-picker and https://developers.google.com/photos/support/updates. Since March 31 2025 the Library API is restricted to app-created content; do not promise automatic full-library or album sync, scrape Google Photos or assume selected base URLs are permanent. If policy permits, explicitly selected/imported media stored privately with expiring access; otherwise use local uploads, native slideshow or approved separate storage. No auto-upload, face recognition, biometric profiling or third-party AI photo access by default. Let parents exclude/remove photos, turn off slideshow for visitors and delete imports. No photos or personal metadata in Git, logs, analytics or public previews.
