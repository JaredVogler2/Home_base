# 05 — BANK: earned XP, savings and reward shop

Integrate QUEST and optional ACADEMY rewards with BANK through a single authoritative ledger. Distinguish lifetime experience (never decreases merely from spending) from available spendable XP/stars. Parent sets earning rates and reward prices; default to a straightforward 1:1 spendable XP conversion on approved tasks. Model grants, spends, refunds and corrections as immutable, auditable transactions, with references and idempotency keys. Corrections may change earned metrics explicitly and transparently; no silent edits. Use integer arithmetic, DB transaction/locking or equivalent, constraints against overdraft, server-calculated balances, clear pending vs available. Provide configurable limits and deterministic rules. Do not model as real financial accounts or external purchases.

Child enters an animated piggy bank/treasure room, sees available balance as visual groups of stars/tokens and goal progress (numbers optional). Reward shop uses picture cards and read-aloud descriptions. Examples: choose family game, choose story, outing, toy or other optional parent-approved activity. Child selects reward, sees cost as visible tokens, confirms via large button and gets pending status. Parents can approve/decline with neutral feedback, fulfill, refund, archive reward, adjust price and define limited stock. Reserve or atomically deduct XP according to documented state machine; ensure rejection releases reserved credit. Prevent double spends and stale-price surprise. Reward affordability and wish-list savings goals should be easy to understand without arithmetic; never make basic needs contingent on points.

Design cooperative family quests only as opt-in, with no sibling pressure. Optional savings jars, visual "how close" guidance, earned/spent history, parent-created bonuses and weekly allowance-like XP (not money). Include analytics of earned/spent/fulfilled, fairness by effort and age without ranking children. Tests for concurrent redemption, refunds, repeated provider events, price change, child role, offline request, disabled reward and audit reconstruction.
